Risk Settings (PAPER)
Position size uses the lower of a risk-based size (Portfolio Value × Max Risk %, divided by the stop distance) and a cap of Max Position Size % of the strategy, further capped per-strategy by its own Live Budget / Live Max Positions below (live) or Virtual Capital (virtual). Saving on LIVE requires typing a confirmation phrase — it directly controls how much real money a single order can risk.

Strategy Runs run any number in parallel — virtual (simulated) or live (real money), independently
Off — no new entries, existing positions still managed to their own exit. Virtual — fully simulated against real market prices, no broker order, tracked against its own Virtual Capital cap. Live — real IBKR orders, capped by Live Budget ($) and/or Max Positions together (whichever hits first blocks the next entry). Any number of strategies can be Virtual or Live at the same time, even on the same side.
