My IBKR Login not tied to Paper/Live below — one login covers both ...
Your own IBKR username/password — only you enter it, no one else (including the admin) sees it. Stored encrypted. IBKR ties every paper account to its parent live account under the same login, so one entry here covers both tabs above.
My Gateway ...
Starts your own IB Gateway processes (paper + live) using the IBKR login saved above. After clicking Connect, approve the 2FA push on your own phone — can take a minute. Once connected, click "Resume Trading" to actually let the bot trade on your account.
